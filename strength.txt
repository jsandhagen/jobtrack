RESUME STRENGTH SCORE: RATIONALE
================================

Status (October 2026): in UAT (src/main/resumeStrength.js). The one-click
optimizer scores every candidate page for strength, which takes "optimize"
from about 250 ms to about 420 ms. That's accepted: optimize shows a spinner
from the click. The current follow-up prioritizes hiring evidence and caches strength reads;
desktop timings still need checking.


WHY A SECOND SCORE
------------------
The goal of the one-click optimizer is the best resume for the posting, not
the best ATS number. Until now it maximized the ATS score and used relevance
only to break exact ties. The ATS score answers one question: will screening
software pass this page? A recruiter then spends a few seconds on the page and
asks a different one: do I want to talk to this person? Pages can pass the
first and fail the second:

  - The summary restated the first bullet ("Led competitive intelligence for
    the CTO." directly above "Led competitive intelligence for the CTO,
    tracking ..."), spending the most-read line on a repeat.
  - On a public-sector role the summary's proof was a banking sales play.
  - Bullets of near-equal ATS value were swapped by score alone, sometimes
    for the weaker one in a reader's eyes.

The fit score doesn't cover this either: it judges the person against the job
from all their documents, not how well one page presents them.

So the strength score measures the page as a recruiter screens it, and the
optimizer now aims at ATS and strength together.


WHAT IT MEASURES
----------------
A 0-1 presentation score (A >= 0.85, B >= 0.72, C >= 0.58, else D).
Inapplicable parts are omitted and the remainder reweighted. It still does
not establish that a candidate meets the hiring bar: read fit and gap notes.

  - Lead (25%): the first two bullets of the latest employment role should
    show complementary proof. For recognized role families, compare their
    combined role signals, sector evidence and demonstrated requirements
    against the best available pair, independently of keyword ranking. For
    other roles, retain the general bullet-ranker comparison. Project bullets
    count elsewhere but do not displace the latest employment role.
  - Must-haves (25%): required qualifications demonstrated by a summary or
    bullet, excluding degree/experience gates and soft-skill-only units.
  - Role proof (20%): actual work matching the posting's function. CI research,
    win/loss and enablement are distinct from partnership operations. Inventory
    planning is distinct from generic analytics; executive-management and M&A
    execution cannot be bought with unrelated impressive figures. Missing
    role proof produces a visible note. Unknown role families omit this part.
  - Summary (20%): relevant work, contribution rather than an objective, no
    repeated opening accomplishment, sector prominence when available, and
    mention of the primary work already demonstrated by the page. A tools
    list alone does not retell an accomplishment. Sector proof no longer gives
    way to a financial-services anecdote on a public-sector page.
  - Results (10%): numbers or scale, with full credit at 60% of bullets.

Repeated accomplishments cost 15% of the total. Numbers never establish
ownership, seniority or experience the documents do not show.

HOW THE OPTIMIZER USES IT
-------------------------
Candidate pages are valued at ATS + 30 x strength. The final summary search
also prefers a clearly better opening (at least 0.15 more summary credit)
over up to five incidental ATS points, preserving matched required terms and
the qualification component. It does not take a strong 75+ ATS page below
75 for that summary trade. Required matches, qualification evidence and notes
are still available separately from the presentation grade.

Within each role, order existing bullets by relevant proof, ownership and
complementary evidence. The second bullet should add a new part of the role's
story rather than repeat the first theme merely because both have numbers.
Summary area names are backed by documented work. A title stays the person's
historical title; dated experience remains total experience, not invented
specialty tenure. A supported B2B SaaS context is retained. SAP or a competitor
name cannot become claimed software proficiency without documented usage or
a listed skill. A relevant summary may name areas without an anecdote when the
only strong anecdote would echo the first two bullets.

LIBRARY EVIDENCE IN ONE CLICK
----------------------------
The shared optimizer accepts documents, and the app now passes its usable
library. Clearly attributable completed work from recommendations, project
write-ups, other documents and cover letters can enter a transient candidate
bank. Candidate and employer must be identifiable under an eligible role;
ambiguous assignments stay suggestions. Resumes supply the original bank.
Writing samples, hypothetical scenarios, hidden work and ineligible roles stay
out. Only posting-relevant passages enter, with one variant per accomplishment.

Each selected passage has a source quote and appears in optimizer notes and
the returned evidence list. The user's saved bank, contribution wording and
historical titles are unchanged. "Supported the AWS partnership" stays
supported; "influencing deals" does not become "closed deals". Cache keys use
actual document content so equal-length edits cannot return stale quotations.

VALIDATION AND HUMAN REVIEW
---------------------------
The two fictional personas cover the original 16 strategy/alliance/analyst
postings and six related analyst postings, including two held-out titles.
A further realistic supply-chain posting tests whether domain proof beats
larger unrelated reporting figures. 114 focused tests across 10 files pass.
They verify the concrete page, attribution, documented contribution level,
hidden/fictional work, source-bank immutability, duplicate prevention, page
fit, independent lead evidence, supported software skills and honest gaps.

Human-facing improvements:
  - Google Public Sector: the opening now shows pitches used in sales meetings
    influencing Army, Navy and DARPA deals. The summary names public-sector
    work, instead of borrowing a financial-reporting anecdote.
  - Workiva/Zendesk: the opening shows Marketplace orders/internal enablement
    and $9M transactions. Co-selling remains a gap where unsupported.
  - Salesforce CI: public-sector CI/enablement proof is prominent and unrelated
    partnership QBRs no longer supply the summary anecdote.
  - Morgan's supply-chain page: inventory/demand-planning work opens the page.
  - Cyera: the summary names business planning and data analysis, accepting a
    neutral keyword trade while preserving required summary qualifications.
  - Chief of Staff: no longer A; missing 12+ years, engineering leadership and
    team management remain visible. M&A is C: research/exploration is not
    completed transaction execution.

All 22 evaluated pages fit one page. Compared with the earlier draft PR,
seven ATS scores improve, 13 stay unchanged and two decrease: Kickboard 91
-> 88 and Cyera 88 -> 82. Required-qualification component scores stay unchanged
or improve across all 22. Kickboard loses a literal communication-skill match
while gaining documented messaging/training evidence; that is a real scoring
tradeoff. Do not compare raw strength scores with the old four-part rubric.

scripts/strength-eval.js produces JSON/Markdown with full generated pages,
openings, evidence sources, timing and remaining gaps. The offline pipeline
also reports strength parts/notes and covers all 39 saved alliance/random
postings by default. Posting analysis and bounded line/pair caches remain
shared per optimization; the evaluation's warm median is about 29 ms on this
executor, not a desktop UI measurement.
